# Lensr

Public repository: `lensr`  
Private/internal companion: `lensr-internal`

Lensr is a direction for LLM evaluation, observability, and harness-style experimentation. The public positioning is around helping teams inspect how language-model systems behave across prompts, retrieval, responses, latency, quality, and operational signals.

## What it signals

- LLM evaluation harness thinking
- Observability for AI systems
- Prompt and response inspection
- Quality, latency, and behaviour tracking
- Foundation for production-grade AI monitoring

## Public boundary

No internal implementation logic, proprietary eval methods, private datasets, prompt libraries, dashboard internals, schemas, or commercial product assumptions are disclosed here.
