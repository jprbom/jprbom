# Agentic AI Workstream

Relevant repositories include `agentic-inbox`, `agentic_security`, `agent-history-protocol`, and selected private agentic experiments.

This workstream explores how AI agents can move beyond chat into structured enterprise workflows with memory, task context, tool use, approval controls, and observability.

## What it signals

- Agent orchestration and workflow thinking
- Memory, traceability, and interaction history
- Human-in-the-loop approval design
- Enterprise control and safety patterns
- Practical agentic AI for business operations

## Public boundary

No private code, proprietary agent flows, prompts, tool schemas, customer data, internal diagrams, or implementation-specific orchestration logic are disclosed here.
